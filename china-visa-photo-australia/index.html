Which centre should I use?

For mainland China visa applications on an ordinary passport, the Chinese Embassy in Australia routes applicants by where they live.

Sydney Visa Application Centre

New South Wales, Australian Capital Territory, Northern Territory and Queensland.

Melbourne Visa Application Centre

Victoria, Tasmania, Western Australia and South Australia.

The former visa centres in Canberra, Brisbane and Perth stopped accepting these applications in September 2025. Diplomatic, official and courtesy applications follow the embassy or consulate process. Chinese Embassy notice on where to lodge

How to start the online application

  1. Open your centre’s website on a computer. Both centres recommend Chrome. Use the visa category checklist to gather your photo and documents, then create an account or sign in.
  2. Open the application form. Use the centre’s online application entry to fill out the form and add your portrait in the photo field. After reaching Confirm Fee, select Submit again. A Pending status means the form is still awaiting submission.
  3. Track the review. Check your account and email for requests to revise the application. Once the online review is complete, follow your centre’s instructions for lodging the passport and physical documents. A completed online review is a step towards the visa decision.

Online JPEG and paper photo sizes

Take a recent colour portrait against a plain white or near-white background. Face the camera with eyes open, mouth closed and even lighting. Leave space around your head so each crop can meet its own framing rules.

Online upload

354 × 472 to 420 × 560 px

Width × height. Use a 24-bit RGB JPEG, typically 40–120 KB. The centre’s guide also sets limits for face width, eye height and the space above the hair.

Glossy paper photo

33 × 48 mm

Width × height. The printed head should be 15–22 mm wide and 28–33 mm high, measured from chin to crown. Use a recent photo taken within six months.

The digital endpoints use a 3:4 ratio. The paper photo uses 11:16. Ask for a separately framed 33 × 48 mm print from the original photo and check the head measurements after printing. Stretching the upload JPEG changes the proportions of the face.

Melbourne’s in-person checklist asks for a recent printed photo alongside the passport and printed Visa Application Certificate. For Sydney, follow the instructions for your application, including any prompt to bring a photo after a failed upload.

What if the portal rejects the photo?

  • Check the file and the framing. Verify the pixel dimensions and JPEG type. If the face is too high, low or close, crop again from the original. For uneven light or a cluttered background, retake the photo. The photo upload troubleshooting guide explains the common errors.
  • If Sydney asks for a replacement after review, open Modify in your account, hover over the photo and select the cloud upload control. Upload in the photo field and look for its green check. That check confirms the upload step. Sydney photo replacement instructions
  • If the portal tells you to bring a photo, Sydney and Melbourne both describe an in-person route after three failed attempts. Prepare a suitable print so centre staff can upload it when you lodge. If you plan to lodge by post, confirm the photo instructions for your case first. Sydney upload FAQ · Melbourne upload FAQ
